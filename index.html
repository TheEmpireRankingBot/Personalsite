Why I build

I got into software sideways — and fell for it completely.

I trained in facilities management at Singapore Polytechnic and learned operations on the ground at Peak Engineering: work orders, site walks, systems that have to keep running. Somewhere along the way I discovered that with AI as a pair programmer, an idea in my head could become a working tool by the end of the weekend. That loop — itch, build, ship, use — completely hooked me.

So now I vibecode. Every project below started as one of my own problems: wanting to invest with more discipline, focus with less friction, react to provocation with more composure. I sketch the idea, build it in fast loops with AI, then polish it by hand until it feels right. These are tools I genuinely use every day — and I'm always halfway into the next one.

  • Build for myself first. Every tool here fixes one of my own problems.
  • Local-first by default. My apps keep your data on your device.
  • Done beats perfect. Ship it, use it, then sharpen it.
