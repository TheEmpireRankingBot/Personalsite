The operator’s edge

Most engineers have never run a building. Most operators have never shipped software. I do both — and that intersection is the whole edge.

Trained in facilities management at Singapore Polytechnic and tested on the ground at Peak Engineering, I learned operations the unglamorous way: work orders, site walks, safety compliance, systems that cannot be allowed to fail. Then I taught myself to build software — and started shipping.

Today I design and build my own ventures end-to-end: an AI-assisted investing copilot with a transparent risk-discipline engine, offline-first progressive web apps, trading automation, and the command center I use to run all of it. No templates, no team to hide behind — just shipped work.

  • Long-game discipline. Strategy over reaction, always.
  • Local-first by default. Tools that respect their users.
  • Ship, then sharpen. Momentum is the only proof.
